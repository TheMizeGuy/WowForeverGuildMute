# WoW Forever Guild Mute — CurseForge project avatar

## Concept

A heraldic heater shield in deep crimson enamel with a bevelled gold rim — the guild —
carrying a raised ivory speech bubble with a long drawn tail. A forged-iron heraldic bend
with a gold chamfer is laid across the bubble, cut to the shield's field at its lower-right
end and stopped one rim-width short of the field's top-left corner. The crest reads at a
glance as "this guild's voice is silenced", and it is built to survive a 50x downscale: the
whole design is four flat shapes whose value relationships (near-black bar / mid crimson /
white bubble / bright gold ring) still separate at 32 px.

## Rebuild

```sh
bash art/icon/build.sh          # from anywhere; paths are relative to the script
```

which is exactly:

```sh
python3 remote_render.py --out master.png --size 1600 --samples 320   # scp + ssh + scp
python3 make_avatar.py master.png                                     # -> avatar.png, sizes.png
```

`compare.png` needs the older render to sit above the new one and is therefore a one-off:

```sh
python3 make_avatar.py master.png --compare <previous-master>.png
```

`remote_render.py` does, against host `mizepc`:

1. `ssh mizepc powershell -EncodedCommand <base64 utf-16le>` to create
   `C:\Users\benme\AppData\Local\Temp\guildmute-icon\`
2. `scp guildmute_icon.py mizepc:"C:/Users/benme/AppData/Local/Temp/guildmute-icon/"`
3. `ssh` → `& 'C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe' -b --factory-startup
   -P '...\guildmute_icon.py' -- --out '...\master.png' --size 1600 --samples 320
   --view Standard --look 'None' --exposure 0.0 --palette crimson`
4. `scp mizepc:".../master.png" .`

Delete the remote scratch folder when a session is finished.

## Render settings

Cycles, OPTIX on the RTX 4090, 320 adaptive samples (threshold 0.01), OpenImageDenoise,
max 8 bounces, filter width 1.25, seed pinned to 0, `Standard` view transform, Look `None`,
exposure 0.0, 1600x1600 8-bit RGB PNG. **11.9 s** for the shipped master; a 520 px / 48-sample
preview is 2.3 s. Re-running `build.sh` reproduces the master to within 1/255 on 372 of
2,560,000 pixels (GPU float reduction order); visually identical run to run.

Nothing is downloaded, imported or textured — every outline is generated by the script
(circular-arc heater shield, half-plane polygon offset, Sutherland–Hodgman clipping) and
every material is a procedural node graph. No text, no third-party marks.

## How the shapes are made

- **Shield**: straight top and upper sides, then two circular arcs tangent to those sides
  meeting at the point; top corners filleted. Extruded and bevelled (10 segments).
- **Enamel field**: the shield outline offset inward 0.15 as the *intersection of its
  inward-shifted half-planes*. Per-vertex mitering was tried first and folds the outline
  back on itself at the shield's point, which silently produced an empty polygon.
- **Bubble**: rounded rectangle with the tail spike inserted into its bottom edge, so it is
  one polygon and needs no boolean.
- **Bend**: a long 45° rectangle clipped to the field polygon (Sutherland–Hodgman), so its
  lower-right end follows the shield edge exactly like a real heraldic bend. The upper-left
  end is a square cut whose position is binary-searched so both of its corners keep
  `bend_inset` of enamel between the iron and the rim. The gold chamfer is the bend offset
  outward 0.05 and re-clipped by the *same* cut, so it wraps the long edges and the
  lower-right end but stops flush at the square cut instead of gilding it.
- **Contact line**: the shield silhouette grown outward 0.062 and parked 0.42 behind the gold
  in a near-black matte. The outward growth has to beat the perspective foreshortening at
  that depth (a 110 mm lens at 9.23 units), or the plate hides behind the gold entirely.

## Polish round — 2026-09-22

Seven art-direction notes on the previous render, and what each one became. `old` numbers are
from the pre-polish 400 px avatar, `new` from this one.

1. **Bubble tail.** Base narrowed from 0.470 to 0.300 and lengthened from 0.487 to 0.560
   (1.15x), apex moved from (-0.570, -0.700) to (-0.325, -0.930) so it descends into the
   shield's taper at 82° with a left lean. Body corner radius cut from 0.215 to 0.148 (-31%).
   The aspect (length / base width) goes 1.04 → 1.87, which is what actually stops the tail
   merging into the body at 48 px.
   *The tail could not reach the full 1.3x at a strong down-left angle: the field is only
   ±0.41 wide at z = -0.90 and ±0.31 at z = -1.00, so a longer, more diagonal tail leaves the
   enamel. Length was traded against lean; the narrowing carries most of the read.*
2. **Bend material.** New `mat_iron`: base colour driven by the position *across* the bar
   through a four-stop ramp — shadow 0.0055 on the lower edge, graphite 0.0225 body
   (renders at about `#26272e`), lit 0.084 confined to the top 20% — metallic 0.62,
   roughness 0.295, thin coat. Gold chamfer thickened 0.030 → 0.050, which is 6.6 px at
   400 px and still ~0.8 px at 48 px.
3. **Top-left corner.** `bend_inset = 0.105`: the square cut is placed by binary search so
   both corners of the iron clear the rim by at least that much — 0.109 as solved, 0.73 of
   the rim's width — and the chamfer stops flush at the cut, 0.074 from the rim. The black
   wedge in the corner is gone at every size.
4. **Dead lower third.** Bubble dropped 0.035 and grown to 0.755 x 0.585 (+1.3% wide,
   +6.4% tall, +7.8% area); bend centre dropped 0.080 and thickened 0.152 → 0.158.
   *The brief asked for a 5%-of-shield-height drop (0.1375) plus an 8% scale. That does not
   fit: the shield's field is ±0.72 wide at the bubble's new bottom edge, and a bubble scaled
   1.08 puts its bottom corners 0.014 into the gold rim (the corner-circle centre lands
   2.182 from the arc centre against a field radius of 2.323). The largest drop-plus-scale
   with a crimson margin left at the bubble's bottom corners is roughly the pair used here;
   the lower third is instead filled by the much longer tail, which is what "so the bubble
   tail lands in the shield's taper" was after.*
5. **Material variation.** Crimson: a smooth-stepped elliptical radial (r 0.52 → 1.06 about
   (0, 0.10) with radii 1.05 / 1.25) subtracts up to 0.32 from the vertical ramp, so the field
   darkens toward the rim without touching the centre. Gold: `arctan2` around the ring drives
   `sin(3θ + 0.75)`, which slides the gold ramp ±0.24 and swings roughness ±0.062 about 0.252
   — three broad specular runs around the rim instead of one continuous one. Ivory: the same
   radial at 0.26, so the plaque is faintly cupped. All of it is low-frequency; nothing added
   is smaller than about 1/6 of the shield.
6. **Backdrop corners.** `bg_edge` 0.016/0.020/0.032 (cool) → 0.058/0.046/0.045 (warm).
   Corner luminance old → new: TL 62 → 71, TR 62 → 71, BL 53 → 67, BR 43 → 62 — a mean lift
   of 13 levels, +19 on the darkest corner. The warm vignette pool is unchanged.
7. **Silhouette.** Key light widened 5.0 → 8.0 and re-levelled to 1010 W, gold roughness
   0.155 → 0.252. Pixels at or above 245 in the top third of the 400 px avatar: 4748 → 64.
   A dark contact plate behind the gold gives a 1–2 px dark line where the rim meets the
   backdrop at 400 px (visible in the rim crops on both the left edge and the bottom point).

### A/B: crimson bend on an azure field — rejected

One render, `--palette azure` (kept in `PAL_AZURE`, re-render with
`python3 remote_render.py --out ab.png --size 520 --samples 48 --palette azure`).
At 32 px the crimson bend and the azure field are within a few luminance levels of each
other, so the bar stops reading as a bar and the tile collapses into "blue shield, white
blob" — it loses exactly the thing that made this candidate win, which is that the crest is
three separated values. The crimson field also stays the more distinctive choice among
mostly-blue addon icons. **Kept: crimson field, iron bend.**

## Checks run

- `avatar.png`: 400x400, mode `RGB`, no alpha channel and no transparency chunk, full bleed.
- Silhouette safe area, contact line included: the object reaches ±0.399 / +0.456 of the
  frame from centre, which stays inside a rounded square for any corner radius up to 0.22 of
  the side (worst corner distance 0.213 at r = 0.22, 0.130 at r = 0.16, 0.056 at r = 0.10).
  CurseForge rounds far less than that. `sizes.png` previews at radius 0.16.
- Value range at 400 px: p01 28, p50 72, p99 252 — a dark tile that is legible on `#ffffff`
  and on `#1b1b1f`.
- Bubble-to-rim clearance 0.040 at the tail tip, 0.079 at the bubble's bottom corners;
  bend-to-rim 0.109 at the square cut (0.074 for the chamfer) and 0.004 — flush — at the
  field-cut end.
- All three Python files compile and have no unused imports; `build.sh` was run from `/` to
  confirm it is path-independent.
- The mizepc scratch folder `C:\Users\benme\AppData\Local\Temp\guildmute-icon\` was deleted
  after the final render.

## Known weaknesses

- A 64-pixel specular core on the upper-left rim still reaches 255 at 400 px. The
  broad blow-out is gone, but pulling that last glint to 235 means dulling the gold past the
  point where it reads as polished metal, so it was left.
- The crimson between the tail and the bend's lower run is still the emptiest part of the
  field. Filling it would mean either a wider bubble (blocked by the taper) or a second
  device, which costs legibility at 32 px.
- The bend's square upper-left end reads as a bar laid on the shield rather than a true
  heraldic bend, which is the unavoidable price of clearing the corner.
- At 32 px the tail is a one-pixel stem. It is visible and directional, but it is the first
  thing that will go if the icon is ever shown smaller.
